Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you get started. Can't find your answer? Email us at support@talathrive.com

Tala Thrive is one of the only global mental health platforms created with cultural competence at its heart. Connect with certified therapists and coaches who understand your culture, language, lived experience or religious beliefs. Whether you're looking for therapy in Hindi, coaching from someone who shares your faith, or guidance on intergenerational trauma, Tala Thrive makes sure you never feel lost in translation. It's support that feels personal, not generic - because we get you!

Tala Thrive is for anyone from a diverse cultural background who wants support that already understands where they come from. Whether you have tried therapy before and felt unseen, or you have been putting it off because you did not want to explain your culture, traditions or family expectations to a stranger, you are in the right place.

Getting started with Tala Thrive is simple and takes just a few minutes. Sign up on our website or download our app to create an account and complete our quick onboarding questionnaire, which takes ~10 minutes. Based on your responses, we will match you with the practitioners best suited to your needs. Review their profiles, select your preferred practitioner and time slot, and click "Book Now" to confirm your appointment. Still have questions? You can always email us at support@talathrive.com

All sessions are conducted virtually. We have developed a simple to use video call solution (similar to Zoom).

Culturally aware mental health care recognises and respects the diverse cultural backgrounds of individuals to provide effective support. It is the ability of professionals to deliver holistic care that factors in all aspects of a client's identity - social, racial, cultural, religious and linguistic needs - while providing them with the mental support they need.
