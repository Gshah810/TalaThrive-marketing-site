We’re Building a Community of Culturally Competent Therapists and Coaches

Did you know that 50% of people drop off from their therapist within the first three sessions?

And this number is even worse for those from diverse cultural backgrounds due to unique challenges such as the chronic stress of experiencing racism and discrimination, cultural identity issues, colourism and more.

When these core issues are not adequately understood, addressed, or often even asked about by providers, our sense of feeling lesser-than and the perpetual other are only magnified and, most importantly, our very real and impactful pain and struggle continues to go unaddressed.

As our world becomes ever more diverse (and more beautiful, interesting, and innovative, among other things, as a result) it becomes clearer how limited current models and approaches to addressing mental health issues are. It also shows how urgent the need for better and more culturally attuned care is.

This is why Tala Thrive supports our clients by matching them with therapists and coaches who understand their culture, language, or religion through video calls and self-led programs.

We are the first and only platform in Europe focusing purely on culturally competent mental health care.

As a therapist or coach, this is a tremendous opportunity to help you grow your own business and practice, and join our mission to change what therapy and coaching can look like and be..

We will also be connecting you to a community of fellow therapists and coaches who align with our vision to share and discuss best practices, provide support to one another, and use our collective experiences to make therapy and coaching better.

We have already onboarded more than 40 therapists and coaches, who speak more than 20 languages between them. You could be next…

We want to do things differently because we truly care - and we hope you will join us on this mission. We want to develop an approach that will be by us and for us.
